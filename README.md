1. Website Overview
The redesigned homepage presents Anfal Movers, a residential and commercial moving and relocation company operating across the UAE. The business provides home and office relocation, villa and apartment moving, packing, storage, furniture disassembly, reassembly, and installation services.

The new homepage focuses on a more distinctive brand presentation, clearer service discovery, and a streamlined quotation experience. It is designed to help homeowners, tenants, families, office managers, and businesses quickly identify relevant moving solutions and request a personalised quotation.
2. Main Improvements
01. Stronger Visual Identity and Brand Differentiation
The previous homepage relied on familiar moving-company presentation patterns, making it harder for the brand to establish a distinctive visual identity.

Implemented improvement: Introduced a more recognisable visual direction through relocation-focused imagery, connected moving illustrations, and a stronger hero presentation to give Anfal Movers a more memorable first impression.
02. Clearer Service Discovery and Customer Segmentation
The previous homepage presented residential, commercial, and supporting services within a broad service structure, making it less straightforward for visitors to identify their specific requirements.

Implemented improvement: Created clearer pathways for residential and commercial relocation, with supporting services such as packing, storage, and furniture handling presented in a more organised structure.
03. More Focused Quotation and Contact Journey
The previous website provided an enquiry pathway, but the information requested and supporting explanations could make the initial quotation process feel substantial.

Implemented improvement: Refined the quotation experience with clearer guidance on the information customers should provide and more visible contact opportunities through telephone and WhatsApp.
3. Design Improvements
The redesigned homepage introduces several improvements to the overall presentation and user experience:
- Visual presentation: A stronger brand-led hero and more distinctive relocation-focused visual storytelling.
- Page layout: A clearer content hierarchy that separates the main services and customer journeys.
- Service consistency: More organised presentation of residential, commercial, and supporting relocation services.
- User experience: Improved content flow to help visitors understand available solutions and navigate towards an enquiry.
- Conversion experience: Greater emphasis on quotation requests and direct contact opportunities.
- Mobile usability: A design direction focused on making important information and contact pathways accessible across screen sizes.
4. Overall Design Direction
The new homepage positions Anfal Movers around a coordinated relocation experience, connecting planning, packing, transportation, storage, and furniture placement into a clearer customer journey.

The redesign retains the company's existing business identity and service capabilities while improving how its value is communicated to potential customers across the UAE.
